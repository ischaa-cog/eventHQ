---
name: Workspace navigation
description: Why workspace switching is accessed through the flat navigation instead of the sidebar header
---

Keep workspace navigation as one flat list. Internal staff reach the workspace chooser through the Client Workspaces link rather than a separate top-of-sidebar dropdown; client accounts do not see a workspace picker.

**Why:** The navigation simplification removed subsection headers and the extra picker above the menu so the sidebar has a single navigation surface. Staff still need a way to choose other workspaces, while a client must remain limited to its assigned space.

**How to apply:** When changing workspace navigation, preserve the internal chooser route and client assignment checks. Do not add a client-side switching control.
