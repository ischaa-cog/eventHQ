---
name: EventHQ brand contrast
description: Visual rule for keeping the app wordmark and browser icon recognizable across surfaces.
---

Keep one cyan-and-ice event/play symbol with a restrained warm accent across EventHQ's visual assets. Use a white wordmark on navy surfaces and a dark ink wordmark on light surfaces. The browser icon uses the same idea in a simplified dark tile, rather than squeezing the full wordmark into a tiny tab.

**Why:** The old white lettering disappeared on the light client sign-in panel, and a detailed wordmark was unreadable at browser-icon size.

**How to apply:** For future brand placements, choose the contrast variant based on its background and test the mark at its actual displayed size before exporting.
