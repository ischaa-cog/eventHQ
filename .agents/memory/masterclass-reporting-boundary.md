---
name: Masterclass reporting boundary
description: Why legacy masterclass reporting remains separate inside Event Tracker
---

Display legacy masterclass records and goals in Event Tracker as a read-only historical section, separate from current event-performance totals. Keep the existing staff-only management route for editing legacy records.

**Why:** The two stores can describe the same event, and combining their totals without a reliable cross-record identity could double-count revenue and attendance. Removing the old client route before displaying the legacy fields would instead hide history.

**How to apply:** When unifying reports or moving editing controls, first establish reliable record identity and reconciliation rules; do not simply sum both sources or delete the legacy store.
